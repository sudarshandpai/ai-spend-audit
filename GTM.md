# GTM — SpendLensAI Go-To-Market Plan

## Problem

Engineering teams at companies with 10–200 developers are paying for 5–10 AI tools simultaneously — GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Perplexity, Notion AI — with no centralised view of what they're spending or whether it's working. Finance sees line items. Engineering sees tab count. Nobody sees ROI.

## Target Customer

**Primary:** Engineering managers and CTOs at Series A–C startups (10–200 engineers).

**Why this segment:**
- They have budget authority for AI tooling
- They're already spending $500–$5,000/month on AI subscriptions
- They feel vague guilt about not knowing if it's worth it
- They're active on LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and Hacker News — reachable for free

**Secondary:** Founders at early-stage startups making their first AI tooling decisions.

---

## Distribution Strategy

### Phase 1: Free tool → organic traffic (Month 1–2)

Launch SpendLensAI as a completely free, no-login tool. The shareable results link is the distribution mechanic.

**Playbook:**
1. Post "Show HN" on Hacker News: "I built a free AI spend auditor — paste in your tool list, get back a cost breakdown + ROI analysis"
2. Post on r/ExperiencedDevs, r/SideProject
3. LinkedIn post aimed at engineering managers: "We were spending $3,200/month on AI tools. I built a thing to audit it."
4. Every results page has "Audit your own team's AI spend →" CTA — shareable links bring referral traffic

**Goal:** 500 audits in the first month. Each result page with a share link is a word-of-mouth vector.

### Phase 2: Lead capture → email list (Month 2–3)

The lead capture form on the results page collects name + email in exchange for a PDF export of the audit. Goal: build a list of 200+ engineering managers.

**Why this works:** The person sharing the results link is already warm. The recipient loaded the results, found them useful, and is now offered a PDF they can forward to their CFO.

### Phase 3: Paid tier (Month 4+)

Once there's an email list and usage data:

**SpendLensAI Pro — $49/month per team:**
- Save and compare audits over time (track spend trends)
- Team access (multiple editors)
- Slack integration: monthly spend digest posted to #finance channel
- Export to CSV for finance

---

## Pricing Rationale

Free tier is permanent and unrestricted — no "free trial", no signup wall. This is the distribution engine.

Pro tier at $49/month is below the noise floor for any company already spending $500+/month on AI tools. It doesn't require a budget approval conversation.
