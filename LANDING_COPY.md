## How It Works (3-step section)

**Step 1 — Add your tools**
List the AI tools your team pays for: name, seats, monthly cost, and what you use it for. Takes 2 minutes.

**Step 2 — Claude audits your stack**
Our AI engine analyses your tools for redundancy, cost efficiency, and ROI. It knows what each tool is actually good for.

**Step 3 — Get your report**
A plain-English breakdown of every tool: Keep, Optimise, or Cut — with a savings estimate and a shareable link to forward to your CTO.
